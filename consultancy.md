Every consultancy engagement is tailored to the client and project. Your
engagement will combine some or all of the following:

* **Analysis:** You can’t fix what you can’t measure. We’ll establish how
  site-speed affects your business and what web performance means for you. With
  that evidence, we can make meaningful improvements.
* **Audits:** I’m a technical consultant at heart, with forensic knowledge of
  networks and web browsers. My audits are famed for being extensive and
  detailed while remaining highly actionable. They uncover everything working
  against us.
* **Implementation:** Performance sprints are a highly effective way to realise
  returns _fast_. I’ll work alongside your engineering team to ship
  customer-facing site-speed improvements that earn you money. I won’t just
  find the problems — I’ll fix them.
* **Culture:** You should never need to call pest control twice. I’ll leave your
  team able to tackle these issues without me. Together, we’ll build a
  performance culture that considers site-speed from the start of every
  project. We’ll make it easy to do the right thing.

I pride myself on working with organisations, not merely for them. I care about
this so much that [I wrote a book about
it](https://gumroad.com/l/questions-for-consultants).

* What is a slow website costing your business?
