This has been a genuinely quiet week in web performance, especially after [last
Wednesday’s](/2026/09/web-perf-wednesday-011-speedcurves-inp-observer-missed-its-threshold/)
investigation of a SpeedCurve bug and several browser updates. The pause is
useful: it gives us time to go back to the trace nobody quite resolved, the
unexplained dashboard change, or the test that still needs a second run.
Finishing one of those investigations will usually tell a team more about its
users than collecting another handful of things to watch.

## Need Help with an Unresolved Performance Question?

Your evidence doesn’t need to be neat before we look at it together. If RUM,
synthetic tests, or browser traces leave you with a result that doesn’t quite
add up, send over whatever you have: a screenshot, a partial trace, an odd
observation, or simply the question that keeps coming back.

I can help turn that into a testable problem and work out what to check next. If
a second pair of eyes would help, [get in touch](/contact/) and start a
conversation.
